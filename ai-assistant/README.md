# Olga's AI Assistant for Teams

Olga's AI assistant helps draft and post Microsoft Teams messages on Olga Albisser's behalf.

## How it works

- The assistant reads relevant Teams context before drafting.
- Olga sees the exact target, message, disclosure, and attachments before anything is posted.
- A message is sent only after Olga explicitly approves it with `send`.
- The assistant posts through Olga's signed-in Microsoft Teams account and reads the resulting message back to verify the content.
- Attachments are staged and verified before approval and sending.

Every assistant-posted message includes a visible disclosure. Olga remains in control of the recipient, content, attachments, and final send decision.
